Coffee is one of the most widely consumed beverages in the world. It is made from roasted coffee beans, which are the seeds of berries produced by coffee plants. The two most popular varieties are Arabica and Robusta, each offering distinct flavors and caffeine levels. Coffee contains caffeine, a natural stimulant that helps improve alertness and concentration. Different brewing methods, such as espresso, French press, pour-over, and cold brew, produce unique taste profiles. Beyond its role as a morning beverage, coffee has become an important part of social culture and is enjoyed in cafés around the world.
