Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and has long fascinated scientists due to its similarities with Earth. Multiple robotic missions have been sent to explore its surface, including NASA's Perseverance rover, which landed in Jezero Crater in 2021. The rover is searching for signs of ancient microbial life while collecting rock samples that may eventually be returned to Earth. Mars has a thin atmosphere composed mostly of carbon dioxide, frequent dust storms, and evidence that liquid water once flowed across its surface. Future missions aim to send humans to Mars, making it one of the primary destinations for space exploration.
