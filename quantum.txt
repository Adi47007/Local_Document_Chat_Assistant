Quantum computing is a new approach to computation that uses quantum bits, or qubits, instead of traditional binary bits. Unlike classical bits that can only represent either 0 or 1, qubits can exist in multiple states simultaneously through a property called superposition. Quantum computers also utilize entanglement, allowing qubits to be interconnected in ways that classical computers cannot replicate. These properties enable quantum computers to solve certain complex mathematical and optimization problems much faster than conventional computers. Although the technology is still in its early stages, researchers believe quantum computing could revolutionize fields such as cryptography, drug discovery, financial modeling, and artificial intelligence.
